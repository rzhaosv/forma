Privacy Policy

Onsite: AI Lab Interview Prep — last updated September 6, 2026

Onsite has no accounts and no sign-in. Your profile, plan, mock transcripts, drill history and stories are stored only on your device.

What is sent to our server

When you build a plan, run a mock interview, grade a drill answer, or sharpen a story, the text you typed for that request (plus your stated role, target companies and background) is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to an AI model (Google Gemini) to produce the response. The text is used only for that single response. We do not store your transcripts, and we do not use them to train models. Everything you see in the app is saved on your device only.

Data that stays on your device

Your onboarding answers, interview date, plan, every mock transcript and scorecard, drill grades and schedule, and your STAR stories. Deleting the app deletes them; "Erase everything" in the Me tab also erases them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives a random app-generated identifier and your purchase status so the app can unlock Onsite Pro and restore purchases. We never receive your name, email, or card details.

Server logs and abuse prevention

Our server counts requests per network address for a rolling hour to prevent abuse. It does not link requests to you, and the counters expire.

What we do not do

We do not collect analytics that identify you, we do not sell or share data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

About the content and the coaching

Interview loop descriptions are compiled from publicly available sources, including company career pages, published interview guides, and candidate reports on public forums. Onsite is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta, or any other company named in the app. Mock interviews and scorecards are simulations generated by a language model, for practice only. They are not predictions, and they have no connection to any company's real hiring process. No offer is guaranteed.

Children

Onsite is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
