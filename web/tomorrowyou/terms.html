What Tomorrow You is

Tomorrow You is a self-help companion for people who want to drink less alcohol. It tracks your progress, offers a morning check-in ledger, a weekly plan and an urge timer, and shows general information about how drinking affects the next morning. It is a motivational tool, not a medical device or treatment.

Not medical advice

Nothing in Tomorrow You is medical advice. Morning scores, sweet-spot estimates and savings figures are estimates based only on the numbers you enter. Do not rely on Tomorrow You to make decisions about your health; consult a qualified professional, especially if you are pregnant or have a medical condition. Stopping heavy drinking suddenly can be dangerous; seek medical advice. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services.
