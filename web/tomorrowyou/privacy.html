Privacy Policy

Tomorrow You: Drink Less — last updated September 4, 2026

Tomorrow You is built to be private by default. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that stores your quit data.

Data that stays on your device

Everything you enter — your drinking baseline, targets, weekly plan, drink logs, morning check-ins, urge timer history and stats — is stored only on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it. "Reset everything" in Settings also erases it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives a random app-generated identifier and your purchase status so the app can unlock Tomorrow You Pro and restore purchases. We never receive your name, email, or card details.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device (daily check-ins and milestone moments). These are generated on your phone and are not sent through our servers. You can turn them off in Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

What we do not do

We do not collect analytics that identify you, we do not sell or share data with third parties for advertising, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Health information

Tomorrow You is a self-tracking tool. Morning scores and sweet-spot estimates are based only on what you enter and are not medical advice. If you are worried about your drinking, or experience withdrawal symptoms, talk to a healthcare professional. It is intended for adults of legal drinking age.

Children

Tomorrow You is intended for adults who want to drink less and is not directed at children under 13.
