Privacy Policy

Cutoff: AI Fit Check & Stylist — last updated September 5, 2026

Cutoff has no accounts and no sign-in. Your application answers, membership card, fit-check history and settings are stored only on your device.

Photos

When you submit a look for review (or attach a look to your application), the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to an AI vision model (Google Gemini) to produce the review. The photo is used only for that single review. We do not store the photo, and we do not use it to train models. Reviews and the thumbnail you see in your archive are saved on your device only.

Data that stays on your device

Application answers, your taste read, member number, every fit check and its result, and your settings. Deleting the app deletes them; "Reset" in the member screen also erases them.

Purchases

Membership is processed by Apple. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives a random app-generated identifier and your purchase status so the app can unlock membership and restore purchases. We never receive your name, email, or card details.

Shopping links

"Find it" opens a web search for a piece in your browser. Cutoff has no store, takes no commission, and does not track what you buy.

Server logs and abuse prevention

Our server counts requests per network address for a rolling hour to prevent abuse. It does not link requests to you and the counters expire.

What we do not do

We do not collect analytics that identify you, we do not sell or share data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

About the reviews

Cutoff reviews clothes, not people. The AI is instructed to comment only on garments, fit, proportion, color and styling. It is an opinion generated by software, for fun and for getting dressed, not a judgment of anyone's worth. Admission to Cutoff is decided by simple rules about how complete an application is, not by appearance.

Children

Cutoff is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
