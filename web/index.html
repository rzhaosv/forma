Small, honest tools for the days that are hard to name.

Forma is a small studio. We make a few iPhone apps and plain words for the days that are hard to name: the year that got away from you, a front door that got heavier than it should be, the thing you did and would like to make right. Nothing here asks you to announce anything. Start smaller than you think you are allowed to, keep at it, and let the next few years say otherwise.

For builders

If you’re building toward a batch, we can help you get in.

Ship weekly, keep a log, bring your draft answers to the wall. We’ll tell you what’s vague and what’s real.

The playbook →

Independent studio, not affiliated with Y Combinator; nobody can promise admission, but a real log helps.

What we believe

  • Things get better more often than they get worse.
  • Most people are one honest conversation from a different week.
  • Small steps count. Two minutes on the porch is a real thing.
  • Nobody is too far gone. What was done can be made right, slowly and for real.
  • Respect comes from showing up for people, not from being impressive.
  • Your data stays on your phone. We are not in the business of watching you.
  • Nothing resets. Missed days and bad weeks are part of the plan, not the end of it.
  • Being known beats being looked at, every time.
  • You were made on purpose, before anyone had an opinion.
  • Every support email is read and answered by a person.

How we work

Three rules, kept plainly

Make something people want

Every app starts as one person’s real week. If it does not help them by Friday, it does not ship.

Ship, then listen

We put it out, read every note on the walls, and fix the next version.

Small and honest

No accounts where we can avoid them, data on your phone, a person answering support.

Whatever this year was, it can be made right. The door was never locked.
