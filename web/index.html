Forma, a small studio

Small, honest tools for the days that are hard to name.

We build small, honest tools for the days that are hard to name. The day the front door feels heavy. The year you fell behind. The week you could not say the number out loud. We think dignity is the starting point, not the reward. We think small steps are the only kind that hold. We do not do shame, streaks that punish you, or apps that watch you. And we think nobody is too far gone: whoever you are and whatever you did, there is a way back, and it starts smaller than you fear.

Find your pathSee the apps

What we believe

  • Small steps count. Two minutes on the porch is a real thing.
  • Nobody is too far gone. A real turning around gets a real clean slate.
  • Status follows service, not the other way round.
  • Your data stays on your phone. We are not in the business of watching you.
  • Nothing resets. Slips, missed days and bad weeks are part of the design.
  • Being known beats being looked at, every time.
  • You were made carefully, on purpose, and nothing about you was an accident.
  • Every support email is read and answered by a person.
