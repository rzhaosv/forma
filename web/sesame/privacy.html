Privacy Policy

Sesame: High-Protein Asian Meals — last updated September 4, 2026

Sesame is private by default. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that stores your entries.

Data that stays on your device

Everything you enter — your goals, saved recipes, weekly plans, grocery lists and cooking history — is stored only on your device. We cannot read it. Deleting the app deletes it; "Reset" in Settings also erases it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives a random app-generated identifier and your purchase status so the app can unlock Sesame and restore purchases. We never receive your name, email, or card details.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is a local notification scheduled on your device. Turn it off in Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

What we do not do

We do not collect analytics that identify you, we do not sell or share data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

About the content

Sesame's recipes and nutrition figures are original estimates prepared by the studio from standard ingredient data. They are estimates, not medical or dietary advice. If you have a medical condition or specific dietary needs, talk to a professional.

Children

Sesame is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
