How to forgive yourself, feel too far gone, a second chance at life

Whatever you did, there is a way back.

Not a half-measure. Not managing an image or hoping it blows over. A real turning around, with your whole heart, toward the road back. For anyone who does that, the slate is clean. Not mostly clean. Clean.

To you, all the way back

The record you keep against yourself is longer than anyone else's. Nobody remembers the thing you said in 2019 the way you do. Nobody replays the night you were not there. You have been your own prosecutor for years, and you are very good at it. Here is the thing about that job: you are allowed to quit it. The case was never as strong as you made it.

To you, all the way back

There is an old story about a son who blew everything, walked home rehearsing an apology, and never got to finish it, because his father saw him from a long way off and ran down the road first. Not walked. Ran. The speech did not matter. What mattered was that he had turned around and was actually coming, all the way, not halfway with one eye on the exit.

To you, all the way back

When a debt is cancelled properly, it is not filed somewhere to be brought up later. It is gone, as far from you as east is from west, which is to say the two never meet. That is what a real clean slate means. Not managed, not minimized, not held in reserve for the next argument. Gone. You are allowed to stop carrying the receipt.

To you, all the way back

The thing you are most ashamed of does not get the last word. It gets a chapter, maybe a long one. But you are still writing, and a person who turns around all the way, gives their whole heart to the road back, and keeps walking it, ends up with a different story than the one that chapter predicted. Ashamed is where you were. It is not where you have to stay.

To you, all the way back

Turning around is allowed at any age, from any distance, after any number of tries. Sixty is not too old. Twelve years is not too far. The fourth attempt is not too many. The door was never locked. You were standing outside it telling yourself it was, because that was easier than knocking. Knock. It opens from the inside, and someone has been waiting near it the whole time.

You are not too far gone. Come home.
