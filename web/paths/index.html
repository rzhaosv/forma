Paths

Plain words for a rough patch.

Seventeen paths, one for each kind of year. None of them will tell you to try harder, and every one was written by people who know the shape of it from the inside.

Start where you are. Someone kept the light on.

Each of these starts where you actually are and ends somewhere better. Pick the one that sounds like your year.

Back on your feet

For the first step, this week, rather than a plan for the whole year.

No. 01

Getting out of the house

You went inside for a reason. The room got comfortable, then small. A slow, honest way of getting out again.

Read this path →No. 02

A fresh start, a bit later

Nobody handed you a fresh start, so you're making one yourself. Practice, patience, and a timeline that ignores everyone else's.

Read this path →No. 03

Making it right, slowly

You did it; that part is settled. What follows: making it right, practice, undeserved good, one day at a time.

Read this path →No. 04

Getting in shape, quietly

Some changes are nobody's business until they are done. Routine, patience, and the parts the doctor didn't mention, kept quiet.

Read this path →

Coming into your own

For anyone who has been looked at, or looked past, and would rather be known.

No. 05

Second from the left

Someone decided early who the pretty one was. It wasn't you. Here is how to be noticed anyway, on purpose.

Read this path →No. 06

There is no clock

They set a deadline you never agreed to. Here: worth that was given, not earned, and does not run out.

Read this path →No. 07

Looking after yourself

You've looked after yourself and done the practice. Now the part after the mirror: composure, presence, being made on purpose.

Read this path →No. 08

Not announcing it

No badge, no title, nothing that quiets the table. Just the work, done well, and learning to stand in it.

Read this path →No. 09

Keeping your composure

They told a version of you; everyone nodded. Keeping your composure, your few friends, and your name, in that order.

Read this path →No. 10

Working out the rules late

Masking works until it doesn't. A way through loud rooms, friendship, work, and ordinary days others never had to decode.

Read this path →

Your people

For wanting someone to stay, and friends who are still there on a Wednesday.

No. 11

Putting yourself out there

Everyone assumes you had a first. You didn’t. It is not over. Practice, patience, and more than one of everything.

Read this path →No. 12

Getting a handle on it

The hunger is good; the object was wrong. Getting a handle on it: routine, clean days, one person who knows.

Read this path →No. 13

The other twenty hours

The floor gave you something real; keep it. The other twenty hours, the morning after, and friends who outlast sunrise.

Read this path →

Out there

For when the news, someone else’s numbers or the fandom got bigger than your week.

No. 14

Turning the noise down

Caring was never the problem; the phone was. Staying awake to the world without letting it eat your whole week.

Read this path →No. 15

Playing it long

Their screenshot is not your scoreboard. A slower ledger for founders: what you built, who it helped, whether you slept.

Read this path →No. 16

A few people who'd actually show up

It was never the crimes. You wanted the table, the code, the men who show up. That can be built.

Read this path →No. 17

Something to look forward to

The fandom gave you a language, a schedule, a crew. Keep all of it. This is about building the rest.

Read this path →
