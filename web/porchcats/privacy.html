Privacy Policy

Porch Cats: Kitty Collector — last updated September 6, 2026

Porch Cats is a cozy cat-collecting game that runs entirely on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads and no server of ours. This page explains the little that leaves your device and why.

What the game stores

Your porch name, fish balances, items, which cats have visited and your Catbook progress are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Deleting the app, or tapping Delete all data in Settings, removes it permanently.

Purchases

Gold fish packs and the Cat Club subscription are processed by Apple. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives a random identifier generated on your device and your purchase status so the game can unlock the Club, credit gold fish and restore purchases. We never receive your name, email or card details.

Notifications

If you turn on visitor notifications, they are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us to trigger them. Turn them off in Settings at any time.

What we do not do

We do not show ads, we do not collect analytics, we do not sell or share data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Porch Cats is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases require the device's Apple ID and can be restricted with Screen Time.
